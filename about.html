About

A small group, working at the edge of what RL can do.

Tau Intelligence is a research group. The name τ stands for Trustworthy Autonomy under Uncertainty — the property we want every system we touch to have.

Our work sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, learned world models, and robotics. We care about the unglamorous parts: stable training, honest evaluation, robust deployment, and methods that survive contact with reality.

We publish openly at venues like ICML, TMLR, RLC, ICLR, AAAI, etc., and we open-source what we can.

Rigor

Strong baselines, ablations, and confidence intervals — not just headline numbers.

Openness

We default to open-sourcing what we can and writing things down so others can build on them.

Humility about uncertainty

Models that know what they don't know are the only ones safe to deploy.

Long horizons

We pick problems whose solutions matter five years from now, not five weeks.
